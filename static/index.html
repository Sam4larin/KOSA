        Devices

        Every device is simulated until you pair it with a real USB board.

          How to connect hardware

          1. Upload hardware/KoSa_device/KoSa_device.ino to an Arduino-style board using the free Arduino IDE.
          2. Plug the board into your laptop's USB port and press Refresh ports.
          3. Choose its port and the pin your LED or relay is wired to (D13 is the built-in LED on an Uno, so no wiring is needed to try it), then press Pair.
          4. Press Test. The device switches on briefly. From then on, KoSa's commands and the floor plan control the real thing.
          No board yet? Run python hardware/fake_board.py in a second terminal, then pair a device with the KoSa test board port. Experimental: not yet tested on real hardware.
